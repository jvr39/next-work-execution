# Next — what we’d change + blindspots

**Team honesty check.** Not a build plan.

---

## Verdict

The core idea is strong.  
The **scope and story** are too big too early.  
Biggest risk isn’t “no market” — it’s building a beautiful everything-app nobody opens every day.

---

## What we would change

1. **Keep “anyone” as the dream. Sell one job first.**  
   Learn on interrupt-heavy people (e.g. CS / sales / SE). Don’t market “for everyone” until one group pays.

2. **Shrink the first product.**  
   First real version: **calendar + one inbox (or paste) + one Next + Done.**  
   Meetings/screen/all-tools/Next-to-Next = later. Pitch the dream; ship a slice.

3. **Don’t lead with “pay with card” until they did real work in it.**  
   Habit first. Checkout after they feel Done on *their* day — not only Joe’s demo.

4. **Name what you replace.**  
   In pitches: “Instead of Motion/Sunsama/another planning app…”  
   If they won’t drop something, they won’t stay.

5. **Plan for a wrong Next.**  
   If priority is wrong once, people quit. Need an easy “not this — do X instead” and fast learning.

---

## Blindspots (where we’re weak)

| Blindspot | Why it hurts |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Sounds like every AI productivity pitch** | People file you under “another to-do” in 10 seconds |
| **Trust = the product** | Reading email/calendar/screen is magic *and* why they say no |
| **Habit change is hard** | Pretty UI ≠ they open it every morning |
| **Cold start** | “Enough for today” / good Next needs real context; empty = useless |
| **Self-serve vs deep setup** | You want low ops; trust/setup often needs hand-holding |
| **Who buys?** | IC feels pain; manager/IT may hold the card and block access |
| **Demo mismatch** | Live demo is CS Joe; story is “anyone” — confusing in a pitch |
| **Wrong Next = death** | One bad call and they never come back |
| **Privacy / recording** | Screen + meetings = legal and creepy fast if unclear |
| **LinkedIn ≠ proof** | Posts aren’t daily usage |

---

## What we would NOT change

- **One Next.** Never a dashboard home.  
- **Human does the craft.** AI suggests; user owns the work. Not a worker-replacement story.  
- **Admin near zero** as the long goal.  
- **Pain is real** — keep interviewing; don’t quit the tree.

---

## Simple picture

```
Dream:  know everything → only execute
Now:    prove one role opens it daily and would pay
Trap:   build all the pipes before anyone sticks
```

---

## If we only fix three things this month

1. Interviews prove **switch + pay + trust**  
2. Pitch names a **competitor you’d replace**  
3. Product story for v1 is **tiny** (one Next + light inputs) — full vision stays the north star
