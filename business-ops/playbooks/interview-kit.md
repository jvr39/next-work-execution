# Interview kit — Next (this week)

**Goal:** 10 completed conversations in ~10 days. Not 4 weeks.

## Who to book (mix)

- 5 Heads / VPs / Directors of CS
- 3 CS Ops / Enablement / Senior CSMs who run the day
- 2 Sales leaders who feel the same “what next?” load

Prefer B2B SaaS, 5–50 person GTM orgs. Unrelated companies.

## Outreach (copy/paste)

**Direct**

> Hi [Name] — I’m researching how B2B SaaS CS teams turn account risk into completed follow-through. Could we walk through one recent account where execution slipped or worked unusually well? 20 minutes this week; no product pitch unless you ask. [Tue 10] or [Wed 2] work?

**Intro ask**

> Who’s the strongest CS leader or CS Ops person you know running a team of ~5–50? I’m doing short workflow interviews this week — intro appreciated.

## Call script (20 min)

1. **Job (2):** What’s your title, team size, tools (CRM, Slack, Gong, tickets)?
2. **Yesterday (5):** Walk me through yesterday morning. Where did the plan break?
3. **Pain artifact (5):** Show me (or describe) one real thing you use to decide “what’s next” — inbox, spreadsheet, Slack, forecast sheet.
4. **Switch (3):** Last time you tried a new CS/productivity tool — what made you adopt or abandon?
5. **Price probe (3):** If something put the single right Next in front of you with prep done, and you still judged — what would you pay monthly for yourself? For a team of 6? Who signs?
6. **Pilot (2):** Would you try a 6-week concierge pilot at $1,500 if we used *your* accounts, not a demo script?

**Do not demo unless they ask.** If they ask, show one Next + one interrupt and say the interrupt is scripted.

## Evidence ladder (strong → weak)

1. Money collected / signed pilot
2. Budget owner intro + agreed price + start date
3. Written “I’d try at $X if Y”
4. Verbal enthusiasm alone — **not enough**
