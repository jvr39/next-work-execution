# Next — packaging map (role doors)

**Rule:** One product. Many doors.  
Not five different apps.

---

## The doors

| Door | Who | What their “Next” feels like |
|------|-----|------------------------------|
| **Next for CS** | Customer Success | Emails, QBRs, 1:1s, renewals, coaching, customer follow-ups |
| **Next for Sales** | AEs / account execs | Follow-ups, deal prep, calls, proposals, CRM hygiene that isn’t the job |
| **Next for BDR** | SDRs / BDRs | Sequences, dials/emails, meeting sets, which lead *now* |
| **Next for RevOps** | RevOps / sales ops | Broken handoffs, data cleanup that blocks revenue, process fires, stakeholder syncs |
| **Next for You** | DTC solo or small team | Creators, freelancers, tiny teams — same loop, lighter stack |

**Primary demo today:** **Next for CS** (Joe’s day).  
**Beachhead buyers:** GTM doors first (CS, Sales, BDR, RevOps).  
**Next for You:** real door, but don’t let it steal focus until a GTM door proves daily use + pay.

---

## How someone buys (story)

1. Land on Next  
2. Pick a door (or “not sure → start with CS demo”)  
3. Feel the day for that role  
4. Solo or Small Team → pay → use  

Same engine underneath. Door = starting pack (priorities, language, sample day, suggested integrations).

---

## What changes per door (light)

- Sample day / demo script  
- Words on the cards (“renewal” vs “opportunity” vs “lead”)  
- Suggested tools (Gainsight/Salesforce vs Outreach vs HubSpot, etc.)  
- Default “enough for today” rules  

What does **not** change:
- One Next at a time  
- Human does the craft  
- Admin near zero goal  
- Approve before send/book  

---

## Pitch one-liner

“Next for GTM — CS, Sales, BDR, RevOps — and **Next for You** if you’re solo.  
Same idea: one thing in front of you. I’ll show CS.”

---

## Build order (when we build)

1. **Next for CS** pack (we have the demo bones)  
2. **Next for Sales** pack (closest cousin)  
3. **BDR** pack  
4. **RevOps** pack  
5. **Next for You** (generic / creator-light)

Don’t ship five full products in parallel.

---

## Interview mix

Talk to people behind each GTM door.  
Ask: “Would you pick **Next for [your role]** or just Next?”  
Learn if doors help or confuse.
