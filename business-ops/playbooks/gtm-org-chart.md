# GTM org chart (full)

**GTM = Go-to-Market** — everyone who finds, wins, and keeps customers.

Use this for **Next for CS / Sales / BDR / RevOps** context. Real companies mix titles; this is the clean map.

---

## Picture (top → bottom)

```
                    CEO / Founder
                          │
              ┌───────────┴───────────┐
              │                       │
         CRO / VP GTM              (sometimes CMO
         (or Head of GTM)           reports here or
              │                     separately to CEO)
              │
    ┌─────────┼─────────┬─────────────┬──────────────┐
    │         │         │             │              │
 Marketing   Sales    CS / CX      Solutions      RevOps
    │         │         │          / SE            │
    │         │         │             │              │
   …         …         …             …              …
```

---

## Leadership

| Role | Owns |
|------|------|
| **CRO / VP GTM / Head of GTM** | Whole revenue machine: pipeline → close → retain → expand |
| **CMO / VP Marketing** | Demand, brand, pipeline *creation* (sometimes under CRO, sometimes peer) |
| **VP Sales** | Hitting quota — new business + often expansion |
| **VP Customer Success** (or CX) | Retention, NRR, health, renewals |
| **VP/Head of Solutions / SE** | Technical win + post-sale technical trust |
| **Head of RevOps** | Systems, process, data, forecasting hygiene |

Small companies: one person wears 2–3 of these hats.

---

## Marketing

```
VP / Head of Marketing
 ├── Product Marketing (PMM)     — positioning, launch, battlecards
 ├── Demand Gen / Growth         — ads, SEO, webinars, pipeline goals
 ├── Content / Brand             — site, stories, social
 ├── Field / Events (optional)   — conferences, dinners
 └── Marketing Ops (sometimes sits in RevOps)
```

**Next door:** less primary than Sales/CS/BDR (more campaign work than “one Next” customer days). Still GTM.

---

## Sales

```
VP Sales
 ├── Sales Managers (by segment or region)
 │    ├── AEs (Account Executives)     — own deals, close
 │    └── AMs (Account Managers)       — some orgs; often overlaps CS
 ├── Enterprise AEs
 ├── Mid-market AEs
 └── SMB / Velocity AEs
```

**Partners with:** BDR/SDR (sourced meetings), SE (demos/POV), CS (handoff), RevOps (CRM/forecast).

**Next door:** **Next for Sales**

---

## BDR / SDR (pipeline creation)

```
Director / Manager, SDR/BDR
 ├── Inbound SDRs     — work inbound leads
 ├── Outbound BDRs    — prospect + sequence
 └── Sometimes dual-role “full-cycle” junior AEs
```

Often reports to **Sales**; sometimes to **Marketing** (inbound).

**Next door:** **Next for BDR**

---

## Customer Success / Customer Experience

```
VP CS / CX
 ├── CS Managers
 │    ├── CSMs (Enterprise / Mid-market / SMB)
 │    └── Onboarding / Implementation specialists (sometimes)
 ├── Support (sometimes under CS, sometimes separate)
 │    ├── Support Manager
 │    └── Support Agents / Tier 1–2
 ├── Professional Services / Services (optional)
 └── Customer Education / Community (optional)
```

**Renewals / Expansion:**  
- Some companies: CS owns renewals  
- Some: Sales/AM owns expansion; CS owns health  
- Enterprise: often **CSM + AE** paired on the account

**Next door:** **Next for CS** (demo use case)  
**Support:** connected system — CSM sees tickets, intervenes when needed; Support owns the queue.

---

## Solutions / Sales Engineering

```
Head of Solutions / SE
 ├── Pre-sales SEs          — demos, POV, RFP technical
 ├── Post-sales / Architects (optional) — complex deploy
 └── Specialist SEs (security, industry, etc.)
```

Reports to Sales, Product, or CRO depending on company.

**Next door:** close cousin to Sales/CS (can sit under Sales packaging early).

---

## RevOps (Revenue Operations)

```
Head of RevOps
 ├── Sales Ops       — CRM, territories, quotas, commissions support
 ├── CS Ops          — health scores, playbooks, tooling
 ├── Marketing Ops   — attribution, scoring, MAP/CRM sync
 └── Strategy / Analytics — forecast, funnel, capacity
```

The “nervous system.” Not always customer-facing day-to-day, but owns whether GTM can run.

**Next door:** **Next for RevOps**

---

## How work flows (simple)

```
Marketing → creates interest
    ↓
BDR/SDR → qualifies + books meeting
    ↓
AE (+ SE) → sells + closes
    ↓
CS (+ Support / Services) → onboard, adopt, renew, expand
    ↓
RevOps → keeps systems, data, and forecast honest
```

## Typical size snapshots

| Stage | Rough GTM shape |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Early (founder-led)** | Founder sells; maybe 1 AE + 1 CSM; RevOps = spreadsheets |
| **~10–30 GTM** | VP Sales + VP CS emerging; small SDR pod; 1 RevOps |
| **Scaled** | Full chart above; segments (ENT / MM / SMB); managers under each pillar |
