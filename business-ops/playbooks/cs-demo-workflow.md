# Next for CS — demo workflow (best practice)

**Purpose:** What a strong Customer Success day should feel like in the demo.  
**Rule:** One Next at a time. Joe does the craft. Next carries admin + triage.  
**Live demo today:** Joe = CS Director (still fine — same loop for an IC CSM with smaller book).

---

## Best-practice CS day (simple)

High performers roughly split time like this:
- **~50% proactive** (risk, renewals, QBRs, coaching, expansion)
- **~30% reactive** (customer pings, escalations — handled, not owned forever)
- **~20% admin** (CRM, notes) → Next should crush this toward near zero

**Morning rule:** Health / risk before inbox rabbit holes.  
**Priority rule:** Renewal risk + health drops + silent accounts beat “nice” email.  
**Support rule:** Next is plugged into Support. CSM can **see unresolved tickets**, what’s stuck, and **intervene when needed** — not drown in every ticket. Default: Support owns the ticket; CSM steps in for relationship / risk / escalation.  
**End of day:** Enough for today ≠ inbox zero.

---

## How Next should order work (priority brain)

When choosing the Next card, prefer in this order:

1. **Revenue at risk soon** (renewal red, exec threat, churn language)  
2. **Calendar hard stops** (call / 1:1 / forecast meeting starting soon)  
3. **Silent / health-drop accounts** that need a human touch today  
4. **Time-sensitive prep** (QBR tomorrow, deck, mutual action plan)  
5. **AI-prepared approvals** (drafts Joe only needs to check)  
6. **Coaching / team** (1:1s, call review)  
7. **Expansion / strategic** if capacity remains  
8. **Admin / CRM** only if it blocks forecast — never as the hero of the day  

Interrupt (Slack/email) can jump the queue if it’s #1 or #2.

## Demo storyboard (~8 minutes)

Use this as the gold path for **Next for CS**.

### 0. Sit down
Joe opens Next. No dashboard to groom.

**Morning briefing (30 sec)**
- Usable time today (e.g. ~5h)
- Biggest change overnight (e.g. Acme renewal now at risk)
- Need today: N items · Finish urgent by ~X time
- Next meeting countdown

CTA: **Start my day**

---

### Card 1 — Risk first (proactive, human judgment)
**Next:** Review Acme renewal risk  
**Why now:** Health red · usage down · competitor named · renewal close  
**Next prepared:** Brief + Gong clip + suggested escalation options  
**Joe does:** Pick the approach (e.g. exec air cover) · approve message  
**Done**

*Best practice:* Risk before random email.

---

### Card 2 — Fast approve (admin → near zero)
**Next:** Approve Globex QBR follow-up (AI drafted)  
**Why now:** Promise made on last QBR; keeps trust  
**Joe does:** Skim · tweak if needed · approve send  
**Done**

*Best practice:* Human approves; system did the dig work.

---

### INTERRUPT (Waze moment)
Slack from AE: customer won’t renew without exec cover **this week**.  

Next asks: **Replan?**  
→ Pull Acme / escalation to front · shove lower work later · keep hard calendar meetings.

*Best practice:* Plans aren’t sacred. Revenue risk jumps the line.

---

### Card 3 — Hard calendar (you do this)
**Next:** Team forecast review (in X minutes)  
**Prepared:** Agenda + forecast deltas + which accounts need a voice  
**Joe does:** Run the meeting (craft)  
**Done**

---

### Card 4 — Batch approvals (admin crush)
**Next:** Approve 3 customer responses  
**Prepared:** Drafts with context  
**Joe does:** Approve / edit / route one to Support if it’s a ticket  
**Done**

*Best practice:* Don’t fix Support tickets as CSM — warm-handoff.

---

### Card 5 — Coaching 1:1 (people craft)
**Next:** 1:1 with Alex (CSM)  
**Prepared:** Last week wins/misses · sample agenda · coaching focus (e.g. discovery on exec calls)  
**Joe does:** Have the conversation  
**Done**

*Best practice:* Managers coach; Next brings the packet.

---

### Card 6 — Parked / enough for today
**Next says:** Urgent revenue + meetings + coaching cleared.  
Globex adoption dig can wait until tomorrow.  
**Enough for today.**

*Best practice:* Not every task today. Protect energy and quality.

## What each Next card must show (demo UI)

1. **What** — one sentence  
2. **Why now** — risk / time / person / money  
3. **Evidence** — health, Gong, Slack, email (fake is fine)  
4. **Suggestion** — optional approach (AI)  
5. **Draft / agenda** — if approve-or-run  
6. **Buttons:** Do it · Skip · Done · Not this (wrong Next)

## Week shape behind the demo (don’t show all; know it)

| Cadence | Best practice |
|---------|----------------|
| Daily | Risk sweep · silent accounts · calendar hard stops |
| Weekly | Forecast hygiene · 1:1s · one QBR-prep block protected |
| Renewal window | 90 / 60 / 30 day plays (risk → save plan → exec) |

Next’s job daily = pull the right play into **one card**.
